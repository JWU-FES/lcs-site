    Our structure

    Three tiers, one team.

    Executive Board

    Led by the President. Six departments run the club day to day, each with a representative voted in by its members.

    Advisory Board

    Former presidents and experienced senior members who provide strategic guidance, mentorship and oversight.

    Projects and members

    Project Managers, Member Trainers and members, who learn and lead on real initiatives.

    The Executive Board

    Six departments, one shared ambition.

    The President leads overall strategy and represents the club. Each department carries a mission. Pick one to explore it.

    Governance

    Elected, evaluated, renewed.

    ElectedBy each department

    Department representatives are voted in by the members of their own department.

    One yearA one-year mandate

    The Executive Board serves a single academic-year term.

    Mid-yearA semester review

    A semestrial evaluation keeps the board accountable.

    StrategicAdvisory Board oversight

    Major orientations and oversight involve the Advisory Board.

    Frequently asked questions

    Before you apply.

    How do I apply?

    Fill in the Google Form during a recruitment wave, then have an interview. Dates are announced on Instagram.

    Do I need experience?

    No. You learn by doing from Day 1, with direct mentorship.

    Is there a fee?

    A symbolic annual contribution applies. The exact amount varies per recruitment wave, depending on the annual action plan.

    Can I change department?

    Yes. Your path evolves with your interests, and we help you find your place.
